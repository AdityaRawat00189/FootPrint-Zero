# DSAR Deletion & Erasure Skill

## Purpose
Enforces statutory consumer deletion rights against commercial data controllers and aggregators under GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and CCPA/CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.105).

## Key Statutory Requirements
1. **Mandatory Deletion Scope**: Covers primary contact information, inferred behavioral profiles, and commercial indexing.
2. **Downstream Propagation**: Mandates that the data controller direct all third-party vendors and contractors to delete records.
3. **Statutory Response Deadlines**:
   - **CCPA / CPRA**: 45 calendar days (10 business days for receipt confirmation).
   - **GDPR**: 30 calendar days (1 month).
4. **Verification & Fee Prohibition**: Must be processed free of charge for verified consumer requests.
